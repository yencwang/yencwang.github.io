Biodiversity of fish in Taiwan

How do ancient and modern fishery practices impact maritime biodiversity? Our research in Tainan Science Park and the large yellow croaker indicates the decrease of fish remain in Southern Taiwan archaeological sites was not a result of overfishing but geomorphological changes, against previous uncritical assumption. Modern fishery, however, accelerate fish growth and narrow their life spans. See The Liberty Times news for more information.
